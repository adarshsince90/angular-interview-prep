Always begin every new topic by providing:

Interview Priority:
- Must Know / Important / Nice to Know

Interview Frequency:
- Very Common
- Common
- Occasionally Asked
- Rare

Recommended Depth:
- Basic Awareness
- Working Knowledge
- Strong Understanding
- Expert Level

For interview preparation, spend more time on concepts than coding syntax.

When code examples are required, keep them small and focused on illustrating the concept rather than building a complete application.

Also indicate whether the concept is sufficient for:
- Mid-Level Interviews
- Senior Interviews
- Lead/Architect Discussions
